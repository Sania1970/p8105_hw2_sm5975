The final combined dataset, `trash_wheel_df`, contains 1269
observations, where each observation is a single dumpster collection by
Mr. Trash Wheel, Professor Trash Wheel, or Gwynnda, with 15 variables.

wheel collected the trash), `dumpster` (the dumpster number), `month`,
`year`, and `date`, along with `weight_tons` and `volume_cubic_yards`,
and counts of specific items such as `plastic_bottles`,
`cigarette_butts`, `wrappers`, and `homes_powered`. The variable
`sports_balls` is available only for Mr. Trash Wheel, and
`glass_bottles` is not recorded for Gwynnda, so those values are `NA` in
the combined data. For available data, Professor Trash Wheel collected a
total of 294.96 tons of trash. Gwynnda collected a total of 18,120
cigarette butts in June of 2022.

variable descriptions, and `na = "."` to point out missing ages at MCI

recoded to labeled factors. 483 participants were recruited.

The first row of biomarker file contains a description of the time
variables, so it was skipped with `skip = 1` to make it more concise,
and this consequently makes the second row the header. The missing
biomarker values appear under both, “NA” and “Na”, so both are
technically missing. If not for that, the amyloid columns would then be
read as character. Variable names were standardized with
`clean_names()`, and the `study_id` was renamed `id`as that was what it
was reffered to on dataset. The data were originally wide, with one
column per visit (baseline, then 2, 4, 6, and 8 years after baseline),
so they were turned into long format with a `time` variable and an
`amyloid_ratio` variable. The resulting dataset has 487 participants and
2435 rows, one per participant per visit. Many measurements are missing
(172), and participants do not all have the same visits, so follow-up is
unbalanced.

Not everyone appear in both datasets. 8 participants (IDs 14, 49, 92,
179, 268, 304, 389, and 412) have baseline demographics but no biomarker
data, and 16 participants (IDs 484 to 495) have biomarker data but no
baseline demographics. The amyloid-only participants cannot be checked
against the inclusion criteria, since their age, sex, APOE4 status, and
MCI history are unknown. This suggests the two files came from slightly
different extracts of the study. Participants missing from either file
cannot be used in analyses that need both demographics and biomarkers,
so they are excluded when the datasets are combined.
